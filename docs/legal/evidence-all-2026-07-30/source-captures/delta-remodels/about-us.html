who we are

Built on Trust, Craftsmanship, and Design

Delta - Bathroom and Kitchen Remodeling was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Lake Forest, Illinois - at 1212 Ranch Road, in the same North Shore community where many of our clients live. We are not a regional chain that travels into this market. We are a Lake Forest firm, and the craftsmanship standard in these homes is the standard we build to every day.

Over the past 39 years, we have completed 500+ kitchen, bathroom, basement, and accessible remodeling projects across Lake Forest, Highland Park, Winnetka, Lake Bluff, and the surrounding North Shore communities. Every project gets a dedicated designer and project manager. We do not take on more work than we can personally oversee, because quality requires attention.

What began as a small family operation has grown into one of the North Shore's most trusted remodeling firms. Our reputation was built one kitchen, one bathroom, one satisfied homeowner at a time. We still answer our own phones, still show up to every job site, and still stand behind every project with a 3-Year workmanship warranty.

Our Values

What We Stand For

These are not slogans on a wall. They are commitments that shape every decision we make, from the first phone call to the final walk-through.

01

Craftsmanship First

We do not cut corners. Every joint is tight, every tile is level, every detail is considered. Our crew takes personal pride in the quality of their work because they know their name is attached to every project.

02

Honest Communication

You will never wonder what is happening with your project. We provide weekly updates, answer calls promptly, and give you honest timelines and budgets. If something changes, you hear about it from us first.

03

Respect for Your Home

We treat your home with the same care we would treat our own. Dust barriers go up before the first hammer swings. Floor protection stays down until the final walk-through. Daily cleanup is non-negotiable.

04

Design That Serves You

Beautiful spaces that do not function well are not well-designed. We start by understanding how you live, then design around your routines, your habits, and your family. Form follows function, always.

05

Fixed-Price Integrity

The price we quote is the price you pay. No surprise change orders, no hidden fees, no conveniently forgotten line items. Our fixed-price contracts protect you from the industry standard of budget overruns.

06

Long-Term Relationships

More than half our work comes from referrals and returning clients. That statistic tells you everything about how we treat people. We are building a reputation that outlasts any single project.

How We Work

Our Design-First Approach

Before we pick up a hammer, we spend time understanding how you live, what you value, and what your space needs to become. The result is a remodel that does not just look beautiful, but works beautifully for your life.

Dedicated designer on every project
Not a salesperson. A trained designer who understands spatial flow, materials, and your lifestyle.
3D renderings before construction begins
See your finished space before a single wall is opened. Make changes on screen, not on site.
In-house crew for core construction
Our own skilled tradespeople handle carpentry, tile, and finish work. No revolving door of unknown subcontractors.
Licensed and fully insured in Illinois
Full liability coverage and workers compensation. Your home and our team are protected.
Premium materials from curated suppliers
We partner with Studio41, ACK, and other leading suppliers to source materials that look exceptional and last decades.
Fixed-price contracts with no surprise costs
Your quoted price is your final price. We absorb cost overruns, not you.
Our Team

The People Behind the Craft

Delta - Bathroom and Kitchen Remodeling is not a faceless corporation. We are a close-knit team of designers, project managers, and skilled tradespeople who genuinely care about the spaces we create and the families who live in them.

Our designers bring years of residential experience and stay current with the latest trends in kitchen, bathroom, basement, and accessible design. Our project managers have built their careers on keeping remodels on time and on budget. And our construction crew? Many have been with us for over a decade, because when you find people who share your standards, you hold onto them.
