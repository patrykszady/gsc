Questions

Common questions about our services

Which remodeling service should I start with?

If both kitchen and bathroom need work, most households benefit from the bathroom first — smaller scope, shorter timeline, and less daily disruption. If one room is functionally failing, that room goes first regardless.

How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?

A standard kitchen remodel typically takes 6-10 weeks from demolition to completion, depending on the scope. Full gut renovations with structural changes may take 10-14 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most bathroom remodels are completed in 3-6 weeks. A simple cosmetic refresh may take 2-3 weeks, while a complete gut renovation with layout changes typically takes 5-6 weeks.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes, we handle all necessary permits and coordinate all required inspections. We are fully licensed and insured for residential remodeling in Illinois.

Can I live in my home during the remodel?

Absolutely. We take extensive precautions to minimize disruption, including dust barriers, designated walkways, and daily cleanup. For kitchen remodels, we help you set up a temporary kitchen space.

What is included in your free consultation?

Our free consultation includes an in-home visit, measurements, discussion of your goals and budget, preliminary design concepts, and a detailed project estimate. There is no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the North Shore suburbs and greater Chicagoland area, including Northbrook, Deerfield, Highland Park, Skokie, Vernon Hills, Glencoe, Winnetka, Wilmette, Evanston, Glenview, and surrounding communities.

What type of warranty do you offer?

We provide a 3-year workmanship warranty on all remodeling projects. Manufacturer warranties on materials and appliances are in addition to our warranty.

Can accessible features look like a luxury remodel?

Absolutely. Curbless showers, comfort-height fixtures, and reinforced walls are standard in premium North Shore bathrooms. Accessibility integrated at the design stage reads as intentional luxury, not clinical retrofit.

What makes Delta different from other remodeling contractors?

We combine boutique design sensibility with hands-on craftsmanship. Every project gets a dedicated designer and project manager. We do not take on more projects than we can personally oversee, which means your remodel gets our full attention.
