The Fixed Cost, Simple and Transparent Remodeling Process

We have made the home remodeling process easier for the clients and have broken it down into steps.

our process

Step 1: Free Consultation and Scope

Our Free in-home consultation includes a visit to your site, discussing project scope, design ideas, the space needed, and the price range. This meeting results in an opportunity for you to ask the questions openly and get the best options.

Step 2: Design & Proposal

The next process is very important, where we will create the layout and show you some 3D renderings for the final design visualization. The budget and project timeline will be finalized after material selections, and then provide you with the final documentation to move to the next step.

Step 3: Permits & Construction

Our dedicated team will help you with Permit coordination. The entire process will include material ordering, scheduling, and active project management while keeping you updated throughout the project. We focus on making the remodeling project successful under the given budget and timeline.

Step 4: Final Walk-Through and Completion

Right after we have completed the construction, you will do a final walkthrough. The Completion review is necessary to know if we have met the standards and your expectations, and the 3-year workmanship warranty will act as a safety net for any issues that might appear over time.

What does a kitchen, bathroom, or basement remodel typically cost?

Project costs vary based on the size of the space, layout changes, material selections, and the level of customization. Across Chicago's North Shore, most Delta Remodels projects typically fall within these investment ranges:

  • Kitchen remodels: $25,000–$180,000+
  • Bathroom remodels: $15,000–$100,000
  • Basement remodels: $35,000–$120,000+

How long does a remodel usually take?

Current construction durations are 8–16 weeks for kitchens, 3–6 weeks for bathrooms, and 8–16 weeks for basements.

Yes. Curbless showers, blocking for grab bars, wider clearances, roll-under zones, better lighting, and appliance placement can be integrated into premium residential design without making the room feel clinical.
