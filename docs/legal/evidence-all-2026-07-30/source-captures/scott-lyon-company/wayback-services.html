Services

Construction

At Scott Lyon & Company we are committed to meeting our clients’ construction and renovation objectives. Our rigorous processes, practices and cutting edge management techniques will help avoid scheduling delays and cost overruns. and will see any project through from conception to completion.

We see our mission as partnering with our clients through the life-cycle of not only a project, but the relationship. We believe in providing a transparent framework in which clients can make informed decisions. A vital, differentiating element of our philosophy is our resolve to align our financial interests with those of our clients. We help manage risk, analyze scenarios and recommend programs all with the goal of realizing our clients’ objectives for their projects.

Maintenance

Our ability to diagnose and solve complex problems is the result of our extensive experience in high-end residential construction. With our expert knowledge and our strong relationships with highly skilled tradesmen, we are equipped to handle any maintenance and repair need.

From plumbing emergencies to party set-up and clean-up, we can provide solutions to any household need. We understand the level of care an occupied residence requires and remain unwavering in our commitment to excellence.
