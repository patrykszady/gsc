About

Our approach to the construction process combines features of both Modern Construction Management and Traditional General Contracting. Our involvement in the initial pre-construction phase is viewed as a critical team member - providing vital budgeting, scheduling, and technical feedback. Even into the construction process, we consider our role as a representative of our client, with all decisions being made as a team and constant effort being made to ensure that our clients' interests are always at the forefront. But we also recognize that our clients look to us for full accountability for the entire construction experience.

Our team offers a diverse and experienced background in both high-end residential construction and commercial contracting. This unique blend allows us to formulate an approach to construction management based on the best qualities from both areas: the attention to detail, the appreciation and understanding of the highest standards of quality, and a recognition of the personal involvement needed in such demanding projects- all required elements in truly exceptional construction.
