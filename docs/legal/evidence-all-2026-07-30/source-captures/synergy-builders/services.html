Are You Considering a Home Remodeling Project?

Home remodeling is a big undertaking. Before starting, ask yourself the following questions. Your answers are important when contacting potential builders; they help determine the scope and cost of your project.

  • What is your desired timeline?
  • What is your projected budget?
  • What life stage(s) do you wish your new space to accommodate?
  • What is your current lifestyle? How often do you entertain?
  • Who will be using the space? How will it be used?
  • How long do you plan to stay in your home?

How Do You Find the Best Contractor for Your Project?

Not all contractors are right for everyone. Before you hire your remodeling contractor, you need to ask relevant questions, view the company’s gallery of work, research everything you can about the company, schedule a consultation, obtain a quote and compare firms.

Whether or not you choose Synergy Builders for your Chicagoland remodeling services, we feel it’s important to offer the following tips to help you select the contractor that’s right for you:

  • Ask for testimonials and referrals, and follow up by contacting clients
  • Protect yourself by finding out if the company is licensed and bonded
  • Learn about the firm’s experience, especially in remodeling projects like yours
  • Ask about any awards the firm has won for design, work quality and more
  • Inquire if the company has an organized and detailed process that fits your project
  • Find out if the company stands behind its work by providing a warranty
