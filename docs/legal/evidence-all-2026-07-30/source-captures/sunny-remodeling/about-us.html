About Sunny Construction & Remodeling

Sunny Construction & Remodeling is a trusted, family-owned remodeling contractor based in Schaumburg, IL, proudly serving homeowners throughout the northwest Chicago suburbs. With years of hands-on experience, we specialize in kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, basement remodeling, and whole-house interior renovations.

As a local remodeling company, we take pride in delivering high-quality craftsmanship, clear communication, and dependable service on every project. From the initial planning stage through final completion, we work closely with our clients to ensure each renovation is thoughtfully designed, properly coordinated, and completed to the highest standards.

Our remodeling services include bathroom renovations, kitchen remodeling, basement finishing, flooring and tile installation, and general home improvement projects. We follow approved plans, comply with all required building codes, and focus on completing projects on time and within budget.

Sunny Construction & Remodeling has earned hundreds of five-star reviews from homeowners across the Chicago suburbs. We stand behind our work with competitive pricing and a one-year workmanship warranty, giving our clients confidence and peace of mind.
