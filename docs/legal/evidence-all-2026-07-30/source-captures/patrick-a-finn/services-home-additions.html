The Leading Home Addition Contractor in Greater Chicago

Expand Your Home with a Thoughtful, Well-Designed Addition

There comes a point when your home no longer supports the way you live. Maybe your family needs more space, your routines have changed, or your current layout simply can’t accommodate what’s next. A well-planned home addition allows you to stay where you are while creating the space you need—without the disruption of moving.

Whether you’re adding a new kitchen, expanding your living area, creating a home office, or building out a primary suite, the goal is not just more space, but better space. At Patrick A. Finn, LTD. we design additions that feel like a natural extension of your home—aligned with your lifestyle, integrated into your existing layout, and consistent with your home’s architecture and materials.

Our team brings experience, structure, and attention to detail to every project, guiding you from initial concepts through construction. The result is an addition that enhances how your home functions, feels cohesive with what’s already there, and adds long-term value to the way you live.

A Clear Process That Brings Confidence to Your Addition

A home addition involves many moving parts, and having a clear plan makes all the difference. At Patrick A. Finn, LTD., our design-build process brings everything together under one team, aligning design, planning, and construction from the start so your project moves forward with purpose and clarity.

We coordinate the full team behind your project—including designers, architects, engineers, and skilled craftsmen—so you’re not left managing multiple relationships or timelines. You remain involved in the decisions that shape your home, while we handle the day-to-day coordination and execution. The result is a more organized experience, fewer surprises along the way, and a finished space that reflects both thoughtful planning and careful craftsmanship.
