A Design-Build Firm Serving Cook and Lake Counties, Illinois

Based in Palatine, Illinois, Patrick A. Finn is a design-build form serving homeowners throughout Cook and Lake Counties, including Arlington Heights, Barrington, and surrounding northwest Chicago suburbs. Our integrated approach brings design, planning, and construction together under one experienced team, allowing us to thoughtfully transform outdated spaces into homes that feel both refined and highly functional.

Award-Winning Design and Craftsmanship

Patrick A. Finn was named a 2025 Remodeler of the Year (RotY) Award winner by the National Association of the Remodeling Industry, Greater Chicagoland chapter, in the Entire House $250K–$500K category. 

This award affirms our commitment to building homes with intention, true craftsmanship, and long-term performance at the center of every decision.
