Our Process

We offer a variety of staff specialists, including interior designers and architects, plumbers, electricians, and remodelers. The design-build approach helps achieve multiple objectives at once: aesthetic and functional quality within budget and timeline.

1. Planning & Estimation

After you contact 123 Remodeling for your free in-home consultation to discuss ideas for the home remodeling project, your personal manager will answer all your questions and prepare a detailed estimate.

2. Design & Concept

Once initial design concepts and layout has been finalized and meet your standards, they are submitted to our architectural staff that will prepare blueprints used by our contractors on location.

3. Project Management & Construction

We will obtain all necessary local permits, and once issued, construction will begin shortly after. Your project manager will oversee all activities throughout the project.
