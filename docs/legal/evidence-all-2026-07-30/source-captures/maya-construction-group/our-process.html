Our Remodeling Process

We understand that remodeling is a meaningful investment, both financially and emotionally. That’s why Maya Construction Group follows a transparent, step-by-step approach designed to keep you informed, comfortable, and confident throughout your entire renovation journey.

Here’s what to expect when you choose us as your remodeling partner:

Initial Consultation

Your renovation journey begins with a one-on-one consultation.

Give us a call or send us an email. Tell us about your remodeling plans and ideas.

We’ll discuss your goals, budget, timeline, and ideas, offering expert guidance to help you shape your vision. This is your chance to ask questions and explore all possibilities.

Planning

Our design team brings your ideas to life with thoughtful layouts and custom solutions. 

You’ll choose materials, finishes, and fixtures that align with your style and budget, ensuring a space that truly reflects you.

Transparent Quote & Agreement

Once the design is finalized, we provide a clear, itemized quote with projected breakdowns and timelines

We walk you through the contract so you know exactly what to expect before construction begins, no surprises.

Permits & Approvals

We secure all the necessary permits and code compliance on your behalf. 

With our deep understanding of Chicago building regulations, we make the process seamless and stress-free.

Construction

With plans and permits in place, our in-house team gets to work.

  • Demolition
  • Framing (to give the new layout structure and support)
  • Rough Work (includes electric, HVAC, plumbing, installation, and closing walls)
  • Finishing Work (installation of flooring, cabinets, counter tops, tile, painting, lighting fixtures, etc.)

Your Dream Home is Complete

Once the final finishing touches are complete, it’s time for you to step into and start enjoying your newly renovated space.

A few days after the work is wrapped up, a member of the Maya Construction team will join you for a final project walkthrough.

Our goal is to make sure every detail meets our standards and, more importantly, that you are completely satisfied with the results.
