Building Your Vision

Milestones in Our Journey

For over 25 years, we at Maya Construction Group have helped homeowners across Chicago transform their living spaces with thoughtful design, expert remodeling, and personal care.

Founded by Udi Yeger and Ofer Gliksman, Maya Construction is proudly owner-operated. That means no middlemen, no sales reps, just direct collaboration with the people who care most about the success of your home remodel.

Built on Family Values

From the start, Maya Construction Group has been a family-owned business rooted in trust, transparency, and quality craftsmanship. These values continue to guide every project we take on.

Serving Chicago Since 1998

With over two decades of remodeling experience in Chicago, we've helped hundreds of homeowners transform their living spaces, earning a reputation for reliability and results.

Specializing in Full-Service Remodeling

We’ve grown from a small operation to a full-service remodeling company, handling everything from kitchens and bathrooms to basements and home additions. All under one roof.

A Reputation Built on Referrals

Our business has grown thanks to word-of-mouth and returning clients. We’re proud that so much of our work comes from satisfied homeowners who trust us to deliver again and again.

Our Core Values

At Maya Construction Group, we believe in honest communication, dependable service, and delivering the highest quality craftsmanship on every project.
