Maya Construction Group: Chicago's Owner-Operated Remodeling Company

Since 1998, Maya Construction Group has completed 700+ remodeling projects for homeowners across Chicago and its suburbs — with no sales reps and no subcontracted middlemen. Every project, from the first walkthrough to the final punch list, is personally overseen by our founders, Udi Yeger and Ofer Gliksman.

We specialize in kitchens, bathrooms, basements, home additions, and full home renovations, and we manage the entire process in-house: design, permitting, and construction under one roof.

As licensed general contractors (#TCG039361), we handle everything from city permits and HOA condo requirements to the structural quirks of Chicago’s bungalows, greystones, and two-flats — so you don’t have to.

Home Remodeling FAQs - Chicago Homeowners Ask

How do I know if it’s time to remodel my Chicago home?

It is time to remodel if your current layout no longer fits your lifestyle due to outdated functionality, worn finishes, or a growing family.

In Chicago’s competitive real estate market, homeowners also renovate to improve energy efficiency, modernize historic 130-year-old systems, and significantly increase property value before selling.

What are the typical home remodeling costs in Chicago for 2026?

In 2026, typical ranges for high-quality Chicago renovations include kitchens from $35,000 to $80,000+, bathrooms from $20,000 to $45,000, and basement finishing from $50,000 to $100,000+.

Factors such as materials and the condition of housing stock can impact your final budget.

Do you specialize in specific Chicago architectural styles like greystones or bungalows?

Yes. Since 1998, Maya Construction Group has specialized in the unique structural demands of classic Chicago bungalows, greystones, two-flats, and high-rise condos.

We are experts in navigating the specific building codes, masonry requirements, and narrow-lot logistics common in historic neighborhoods like Lincoln Park, Logan Square, and the West Loop.

What is the benefit of hiring an owner-operated remodeling company?

As an owner-operated firm, every project at Maya Construction Group is personally overseen by our founders, Udi Yeger and Ofer Gliksman.

Unlike larger companies that use “newbie” sales reps, you receive direct owner accountability, wisdom, and efficiency from initial consultation to final walkthrough, ensuring your project stays on time and within budget.

Does Maya Construction handle city permits and structural inspections?

Yes. Maya Construction Group holds Chicago General Contractor License #TCG039361 and provides full-service permit management. we handle all structural drawings, city applications, and inspections with the Chicago Department of Buildings.

This ensures your remodel is 100% compliant with local zoning and safety standards without you managing the paperwork.

Can I live in my home during a major kitchen or bathroom renovation?

In most cases, yes. We plan our remodels to minimize disruption by maintaining access to essential rooms and utilizing professional dust barriers.

Our crews perform daily on-site cleanups to keep your home safe for your family.

For larger “gut” renovations or major home additions, we may suggest temporary relocation for your comfort.
