Our Process

Come Visit Our Showrooms in Niles & Palatine

By visiting one of our two locations, you’ll meet with one of our kitchen remodeling experts to get the ball rolling on a perfect solution for your kitchen. Before stopping by, consider taking a few simple steps to get ready for your design appointment:

• Measure Your Kitchen – Take general measurements of the entire area you wish to remodel. Be sure to include windows, walls, doors, and appliances!
• Draw A Floor Plan – By drafting a simple floor plan, our designers can be ensured of a clear understanding of your space.
• Feel Free To Take Lots Of Pictures – The more photos you take, the better we can visualize & plan together.
• DON’T WORRY – We will be visiting the home to confirm all details and dimensions.

It’s your dream kitchen, how do you want it to look and function? Take some time to consider your new kitchen’s style and the kind of features you’d like it to include.
