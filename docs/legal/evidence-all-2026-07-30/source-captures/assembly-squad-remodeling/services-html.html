Basement Finishing

Finishing a basement in Chicago means dealing with moisture — there's no way around it. We start every project with a thorough assessment and address any water issues before building out walls. Basement remodeling in Chicago runs $55–$140 per square foot, and under the city's new ADU ordinance we can even convert your basement into a legal rental apartment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most Chicago kitchen remodels take 6-12 weeks from demolition to completion. Simple refreshes take 4-6 weeks, while full gut renovations with layout changes need 10-16 weeks. Timeline depends on permit approval, material lead times, and scope.
Yes, most remodeling projects in Chicago require permits for electrical, plumbing, or structural work. As your general contractor, Assembly Squad handles all permit applications and city inspections as part of our service.
Chicago bathroom remodels range from $15,000-$60,000 depending on scope. Our all-inclusive Bathroom Refresh Package is $22,500 and includes design, demolition, new tub, tile, fixtures, vanity, and painting.
Chicago basement remodeling costs $55–$140 per square foot — a 600 sq ft basement runs $35,000-$55,000 for basic finishing and up to $150,000+ for luxury suites. Waterproofing is included in every project. See our full basement remodeling pricing guide for details.
Absolutely. We specialize in Chicago condo renovations and have worked with most major buildings. We handle all HOA paperwork, insurance certificates, elevator reservations, and work within building construction hours.
We serve all of Chicago including Gold Coast, Lincoln Park, River North, Streeterville, Lakeview, Wicker Park, Bucktown, West Loop, and the North Shore suburbs including Evanston, Wilmette, Winnetka, Highland Park, and Lake Forest.

Yes, we offer free in-home consultations for all Chicago-area homeowners. We'll visit your home, discuss your vision, assess the space, and answer all your questions with no pressure or obligation.
