Frequently Asked Questions

  • What areas do you serve for bathroom remodeling?

    We specialize in bathroom remodeling in Lake Zurich and the surrounding communities. If you’re searching for “bathroom remodeling near me,” we’re your local experts.

  • How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Lake Zurich?

    Bathroom remodel costs vary based on size and materials. On average, a full remodel ranges from $15,000–$40,000, with smaller updates starting around $5,000

  • What is included in a typical bathroom remodel?

    • Design planning and permitting
    • Demolition and disposal
    • Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC
    • Waterproofing, tiling, fixtures, and finishes
    • Final inspection and cleanup
    • We manage all trades for a smooth, hassle-free project.

  • How long does a bathroom remodel take?

    Most projects take 4–6 weeks, depending on the scope and permit timelines. We provide a clear schedule so you know what to expect.

  • Can you remodel a bathroom without relocating plumbing?

    Yes! We can often keep plumbing in its existing location, which helps save time and money.

  • Do you offer design services and help with material selection?

    Absolutely. We guide you through choices for finishes, fixtures, flooring, lighting, storage solutions, and more. We also offer upgrades like heated floors and steam showers.

  •  Is proper ventilation included

    Yes. Every remodel includes code-compliant ventilation to prevent moisture problems and mold.

  • Do I need permits for a bathroom remodel?

    Most full remodels require local permits. We handle all permitting with Lake Zurich authorities for you.

  •  What sets your remodels apart?

    • Local focus and expertise in Lake Zurich
    • High-quality materials and licensed trades
    • Clear communication and dedicated support
    • Attention to detail and durability

  • How do I get started?

    1. Contact us for a free consultation
    2. We assess your bathroom and discuss your goals
    3. You receive a clear, itemized proposal
    4. Once you’re ready, we schedule your project and begin!
