Home Addition Services

Expert Design-Build Remodeling in Chicagoland, the North Shore, & the Northwest Suburbs

More Room, Same Home You Love

A home addition lets you add the space your family needs without leaving the neighborhood, the schools, or the home you've already built a life in. It's one of the most effective ways to extend the value of a home you plan to keep for the long haul, especially for growing families, multigenerational living, or that long-overdue primary suite.

Skor Construction designs and builds home additions across the Northwest Suburbs, North Shore, and Chicagoland, from single-room bump-outs to full second-story builds.

Every addition we take on is planned around two equally important goals: giving you the new space your family needs, and tying that space into the existing home so seamlessly that no one can tell where the original house ends and the new one begins.

A New Wing, A Second Story, Or Just A Little More Breathing Room

Every addition project is shaped by what your home is missing, what the lot can support, and how your family plans to use the new space.

Some clients come to us needing a full second story for a growing family. Others are adding a primary suite that the original home was never designed to include.

Whatever the scope, the goal is always the same: more space your family actually needs, designed and built to look like a natural part of the home you already love. The most common addition types we design and build include:

  • Rear Additions
  • Primary Suite Additions
  • In-Law & Guest Suites
  • Side & Bump-Out Additions
  • Garage & Above-Garage Additions
  • Kitchen & Great Room Expansions
  • Home Office Additions

Why Homeowners Hire Skor For Additions.

An addition is one of the most technically complex projects you can take on in a home. The structural, mechanical, and design coordination is what separates an addition that looks like it always belonged from one that always looks added on.

Built To Blend With The Original Home

A great addition shouldn't announce itself. We pay close attention to rooflines, siding, window proportions, trim profiles, and interior transitions so the new space feels like a natural part of the home, inside and out.

Strong Structural Foundations

Additions live or die on the structural side of the project: foundations, framing, beam transfers, roof tie-ins, and load paths. We coordinate with structural engineers on every project and execute the work with the precision that the long-term integrity of the home requires.

Permit And Zoning Experience

Additions face stricter zoning, setbacks, and permitting requirements than most interior remodels. We pull permits, manage variances when needed, and work through municipal review so the project clears the approval process without slowing the schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Additions

How Long Does a Home Addition Take?expand_more

Most home additions take between 4 and 9 months of active construction, depending on size, complexity, and whether the addition involves structural changes to the existing home. Smaller bump-outs sit on the shorter end, while full second-story or multi-room additions take longer.

Design, permits, and engineering typically add several months before construction begins. 

How Much Does a Home Addition Cost?expand_more

Home additions with Skor typically range from $80,000 to $400,000 and up, depending on size, structural complexity, and finish level.

Second-story additions and full primary suite additions land at the higher end because of the structural and roof work involved, while bump-outs and smaller additions sit lower. Every proposal includes design, permits, labor, and materials. 

Can We Live in the House During an Addition?expand_more

In most cases, yes. Additions are usually built on the outside of the home, so the existing living space stays largely intact during construction.

There may be short stretches where dust, noise, or utility shutoffs create disruption, especially when the new space ties into the existing structure, and we plan those phases carefully so you know what to expect. 

Will the Addition Match the Existing House?expand_more

That's one of our top priorities on every addition.

We match rooflines, siding, window styles, trim profiles, and interior finishes so the new space reads as part of the original home. The goal is for guests to walk through your home a year later and not be able to tell which part was added.

Our Design-Build Process

1 | Plan

It starts with a quick discovery call and an in-person visit, where we walk the space, talk through what you want, and pressure-test ideas against your budget. Within about a week, we send a written investment range so you can decide whether to move forward before anyone designs in a vacuum.

2 | Design/Proposal

We move into paid design with our architect and interior design partners, producing real floor plans, 3D renderings, and guided selections for finishes and materials. We coordinate the trades and handle permitting, then close with a detailed, fixed-price construction proposal covering scope, allowances, and schedule.

3 | Build

With your approval, we start construction, led by a dedicated project manager with weekly check-ins, so you always know what's happening and what's next. You leave with a finished space, a written workmanship warranty, and a real human to call if anything needs attention down the road.
