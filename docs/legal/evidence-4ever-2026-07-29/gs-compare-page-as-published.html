How GS Construction compares to 4Ever Remodeling

4Ever Remodeling is a design-build firm serving Chicago and its suburbs since 2011, with a materials design studio, a named 7-step process, and separate consultants, designers, and project managers each owning a phase. The difference with GS Construction comes down to who you work with day to day: the owners, Greg and Patryk Szady, run your project from first call to final walkthrough instead of handing you between team roles, and labor pricing stays transparent with no middleman markup.

How to decide

  1. Request itemized estimates from at least two contractors.
  2. Ask whether you can bring your own designer or architect.
  3. Confirm who self-performs the work vs. who is subcontracted and marked up.
  4. Confirm who pulls permits and how inspections are scheduled.
  5. Ask who your single point of contact is — and whether it changes each phase.
  6. Ask whether you get a live portal for your schedule, change orders, and balances.

Is GS Construction a good alternative to 4Ever Remodeling?

If you want to work directly with the owners and keep control of your design and materials, GS Construction is a strong alternative to 4Ever Remodeling. Greg and Patryk Szady are a father-son team who run every project from the first call to the final walkthrough — there is no rotating cast of coordinators, and you can bring your own designer or architect (or be your own) and shop your own materials from our trusted material sources.

How does GS Construction's pricing compare to 4Ever Remodeling?

GS Construction gives you an itemized, transparent estimate with no labor marked up through layers of middlemen, so you can see exactly what you are paying for. Pricing on any remodel depends on scope and finishes, so the best way to compare is to request an itemized estimate from GS Construction and from 4Ever Remodeling and put them side by side.

What areas does GS Construction serve?

GS Construction serves the northwest Chicago suburbs — including Arlington Heights, Palatine, Schaumburg, Barrington, and surrounding communities — for kitchen, bathroom, and whole-home remodels, additions, basements, exteriors, and mudrooms.

Can I bring my own designer or buy my own materials?

Yes. You can collaborate with the independent designer or architect of your choice, or design the project yourself. We point you to our trusted material sources, follow your requirements, and install the materials you purchase — your design, your decisions.
